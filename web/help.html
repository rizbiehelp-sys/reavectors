How to use ReaVector

Every tool is on its own page. Local tools run on CPU in the browser; image generation runs on Cloudflare; heavy 3D/Blender work runs on a manual GPU worker.

1 · 2D Vectorizer (/)

Transparent PNG/JPG → clean SVG. Upload → adjust colours/detail → Refine (10–120 s) → export SVG / PDF / EPS / AI. AI is a PDF-compatible file (opens in Illustrator/Inkscape), not native Illustrator data.

2 · Image Studio (/image.html)

  • Main image = your product (kept exact). References = extra angles/style (up to 3).
  • Preserve original product ON → the app cuts out your product and puts it on a new AI background — your label/logo is never redrawn.
  • Output → Transparent PNG gives your product with a real alpha channel (checkerboard).
  • No main image → text-to-image (type a prompt).
  • Runs on Cloudflare (free tier). No Kaggle needed here.

3 · Video (/video.html)

Text→Video or Image→Video (Wan/LTX, with LoRA support). Needs a GPU worker; shows queued until a session is started.

4 · AI 3D (/model3d.html)

Photo → real GLB. Viewer supports orbit, zoom, Front/Back/Left/Right, Fullscreen, lighting presets, shadows, and 360° spin. Drag-rotate; download the GLB.

5 · Product 3D (/product3d.html)

  • Upload a product photo — or a cut-out PNG (remove.bg) with transparency; both work.
  • Shape preset: Milk bottle · Juice/water (slim) · Jar · Can · (or the photo's own silhouette).
  • Uses your real label artwork (never AI text) and wraps it on the model.
  • 360° spin preview, Download .glb, or Open a .glb file.

Tips & troubleshooting

  • If a page looks stale: Ctrl+Shift+R (hard refresh).
  • Cut-outs look best on plain backgrounds; a transparent PNG works without background removal.
  • One photo can't reveal unseen sides — those are marked inferred.
  • Brand names, logos and printed text are never generated by AI.
